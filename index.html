Questions? We've got answers

Frequently asked questions

Where do you provide care? +
We provide 100% telehealth psychiatric care to patients located anywhere in Texas. All you need is a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera.
Do you accept insurance? +
Yes — we accept most major insurance plans, including Aetna, United Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Oscar, Cigna, Carelon and more. Share your insurance details when you book and our team will verify your coverage.
How soon can I be seen? +
We offer flexible scheduling with appointments often available the same week. Submit the booking form and our team will reach out promptly to confirm your visit.
Do I need a referral? +
No referral needed. You can book directly with one of our board-certified providers.
What happens at my first appointment? +
Your first visit is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation (typically 45–60 minutes). Your provider listens to your full story, answers your questions, and builds a treatment plan with you — including medication if clinically appropriate.
What conditions do you treat? +
Our providers treat anxiety, depression, ADHD, PTSD, insomnia and sleep issues, bipolar disorder, OCD, and other mood and mental health conditions — including medication management when appropriate.
Can you prescribe medication? +
Yes. Our board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioners can evaluate, diagnose, and prescribe and manage medications as part of your personalized treatment plan when clinically appropriate.
Is my visit private? +
Absolutely. All visits are conducted over a secure, confidential video connection, and your information is kept private and protected.
